# A Feast For Odin component / size manifest

All measurements are in mm.

Box inner dimensions: 216 width, 305 length, 117.5 height
Height is flexible, as we can have the lid not close all the way if needed.

For the goods and money, we'd like to have everything divided into 2 symmetric
sets of inserts, for easy access while playing. This is not needed for anything
else.

## Non-cardboard pieces

### Wooden goods

All 11 mm height.

* ore: 11 mm cube, 40x
* stone: teardrop, 20 length, 13 width, 24x
* wood: 21 length, 13.5 width, 32x
* vikings: 21 x 13, 12x in each of 4 colors
* 1 moose
* 1 round tracking cube (same as ore)

### Cards

Cards are sleeved; occupation cards are divided into setup and in-game. The setup
ones just need a place to live where we'll pull the whole deck at once, other
decks it would be nice to have something that doubles as a deck-holder in game to
pull 1 card at a time from.

Sleeved cards are 46 x 72

Deck heights:
* Setup occupation cards: 23
* In-game occupation cards: 72
* Weapon cards: 24

## Cardboard pieces

Cardboard is ~2.25 thick (measured by using calipers on a stack of 8 and dividing).

Islands: 8x, 129 square

Buildings: 139 x 54.5. There are 6x for setup, 11 during game.

Mountain strips: 30 wide, 8x at 169 length, 1x at 192

Player boards: 299 x 190, 4x

Round overview: 96 x 224

Action board, in 3 pieces, all 266 long. Widths: 134, 162, 183

Boat supply: 151 x 78. But I'd like to replace this with our insert to store the
boats; we would want to print the prices (3, 5, 8) on it.

VP bonus: 25 mm square, 2x "4 vp", 6x "3 vp", 8x "2 vp"

Boats, in tiles: 8x 1x4, 10x 2x3, 12x 2x4, 10x 2x4
